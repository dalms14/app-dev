# How to Train Your Dragon

*This animated fantasy film* tells the story of **Hiccup**, an awkward teenage Viking who befriends an injured Night Fury, which he names **Toothless**. The story is about bridging the divide between humans and dragons.

## Key Elements

1.  **Main Character:** Hiccup
2.  **Dragon:** Toothless
3.  **Setting:** The island of Berk

### Why I Love It

- The bond between the characters is **incredible**.
- The flight scenes are *amazing*.
- The music is **epic**.

## A Memorable Quote
> "Thank you for nothing, you useless reptile."
